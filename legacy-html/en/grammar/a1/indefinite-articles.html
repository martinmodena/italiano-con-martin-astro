Indefinite articles

When do we use them?

We use indefinite articles when we talk about a person or thing that is not precise or mentioned for the first time.

Ho un libro.
Vedo uno studente.
Cerco una casa.
Conosco un’amica.

🇬🇧 Why "a friend" but "a friend"?
With the masculine a he doesn't want the apostrophe. With the feminine before the vowel we use one.

Remember: there is no direct plural of un, uno and una. We will use the plural later gods, gods, gods.
