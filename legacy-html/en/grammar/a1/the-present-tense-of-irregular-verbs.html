The present tense of irregular verbs

Some very common verbs do not follow the rule completely. Learn them in small groups: go, do, stay, give, come, go out and know.

Simple idea

Irregular verbs are widely used verbs. That's why it's best to learn them in short sentences, not just as a list.

go
I go to school.
do
I have breakfast.
stay
I'm fine.
come
I'm coming to class.

Tip: first learn the shapes with I, you and us. They are the most useful in conversation.
