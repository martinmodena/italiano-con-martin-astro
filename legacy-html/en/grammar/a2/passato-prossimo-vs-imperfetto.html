Grammar A2

Passato prossimo vs imperfetto

Learn to choose between completed action, habit, description and ongoing situation.

16 minutes12 exercisesLevel A2
ChoiceComparisonsErrorsExercises
PDF A2

The main difference

Present perfect
Finite action, precise event.
Yesterday I studied Italian.
Imperfect
Habit, description, situation.
As a child I studied little.
Event
At eight I went out.
Context
It was late and raining.

Useful trick: if you can say "what happened?", you often use the present perfect. If you describe "what was the situation like?", you often use the imperfect tense.

Clear comparisons

Mini story

When I lived in Rome, I took often the bus. One day I met an old friend and we talked for an hour.

Errato: Ieri andavo al cinema.Meglio: ieri sono andato al cinema.
Errato: Era caduto mentre camminava.Meglio A2: e caduto mentre camminava.
Errato: Quando ho avuto 10 anni...Meglio: quando avevo 10 anni...
