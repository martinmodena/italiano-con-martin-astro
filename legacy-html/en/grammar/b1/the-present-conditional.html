The present conditional

Learn to speak kindly: I wish, you could, I would like, you should.

How is it formed?

The present conditional uses a similar base to the future, but with different endings.

When do we use it?

Kind requests
Would you tell me your name?
Wishes
I would like to speak better Italian.
Advice
You should read every day.
Light hypotheses
I would go by train, not by car.

Very useful: to book, ask for information or speak kindly, the conditional immediately changes the tone.

Common mistakes

Errato: io vorrebbe.Corretto: io vorrei.
Errato: potresti aiutarmi? detto senza tono gentile.Meglio: Potresti aiutarmi, per favore?
Errato: noi parleriamo.Corretto: noi parleremmo.
Errato: loro andarebbero.Corretto: loro andrebbero.
