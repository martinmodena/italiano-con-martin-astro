Italian A1 readings: short stories for beginners

Stories in simple Italian for those just starting: short sentences, everyday words and one structure to learn in every text. Each story has questions, useful words and a free PDF.

All levelsA1A2B1B2C1

What you’ll find

Texts of 100 to 400 words in the present tense, with lots of dialogue. Under each text: useful words, questions and a PDF to print.

How to read

Read everything once without a dictionary. Then read it again, look up only the words that block the story and answer the questions. Finally, read it aloud.

A1 grammar

Review the present tense, irregular verbs, c’è and ci sono and articles: these are the structures you will find in these stories.
