When do we use the imperfect?

Habits in the past
As a child I played in the garden.
Descriptions
The house was small but bright.
Actions in progress
While I was studying, Marco called.
Age, time, time
I was ten years old. It was eight o'clock. It was raining.

How does an Italian really speak?
To tell your life, the imperfect is essential: When I lived in Argentina, I often spoke Italian with my grandmother.

Mistakes that almost everyone makes

Errato: io era stanco.Corretto: io ero stanco.
Errato: noi parlava.Corretto: noi parlavamo.
Errato: quando ero bambino, sono giocato.Corretto: quando ero bambino, giocavo.
Errato: facevo una pizza ieri.Meglio: ieri ho fatto una pizza.
