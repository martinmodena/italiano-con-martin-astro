Grammar A1

Gender and number of Italian nouns

Learn to recognize masculine, feminine, singular and plural.

The basic rule

FinalUsuallyPlural
-omasculine-i
-afeminine-e
-emasculine or feminine-i
the booko → the booksi
the housea → the housese
the restaurante → the restaurantsi
the nighte → the nightsi

Watch out for exceptions

la mano → hands
the problem → the problems
the photo → the photos
coffee → coffees

Nouns that end in a stressed vowel or consonant often do not change in the plural: the city → the cities, the bar → the bars.

Common mistakes

❌ the house
✓ houses
❌ I book them
✓ books
❌ the problems
✓ the problems
❌ coffees
✓ coffees
