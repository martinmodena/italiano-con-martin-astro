Italian present indicative: regular verbs

When do we use the present tense?

Now
I talk to Maria.
Habit
I study every evening.
General truth
Rome is in Italy.

To conjugate a regular verb we take away -are, -ere or -ire and add the correct ending.

Remember: with us the ending is always -let's go. Some verbs in -ire, how clean, understand, finish e prefer, they add -isc- with I, you, he/she e them: I clean, clean, clean, clean. With us e you remain regular: let's clean, clean.

How does an Italian really speak?

I work from home.
What do you eat?
We sleep little.

In Italian the pronoun is often not necessary: we say normally “I speak Italian”, not always “I speak Italian”.

Common mistakes

Correct: I speak

Correct: We believe
