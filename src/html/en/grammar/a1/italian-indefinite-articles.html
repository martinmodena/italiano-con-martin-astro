When do we use them?

We use indefinite articles when we talk about a person or thing that is not precise or mentioned for the first time.

Ho un libro.
Vedo uno studente.
Cerco una casa.
Conosco un’amica.

🇮🇹 Why un amico but un’amica?
In the masculine, un takes no apostrophe. In the feminine, before a vowel, we use un’.

Complete table

FormWhenExample
unmasculine before an ordinary consonant or a vowelun libro, un amico
unomasculine before s + consonant, z, ps, gn, x, yuno studente, uno zaino
unafeminine before a consonantuna casa
un’feminine before a vowelun’amica

Remember: there is no direct plural of un, uno and una. We will use the plural later dei, degli, delle.
