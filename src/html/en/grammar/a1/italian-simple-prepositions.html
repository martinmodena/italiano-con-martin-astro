Grammar A1

Italian simple prepositions

Learn to choose of, to, from, in, with, on, for, between and between.

What do they indicate?

of
possession or origin
Anna's book.
a
direction or place
I'm going to Rome.
from
origin or person
I come from Milan.
in
place or medium
I live in Italy.
with
company or instrument
I'm going out with Luca.
up
position or topic
The book is on the table.
for
purpose or destination
Study for the exam.
between / between
time or location
I'll be there in two hours.

How does an Italian really speak?

I'm at home.
I go to the office.
I leave for Rome.

Some expressions are learned as fixed groups: at home, in the center, at work, from Marco.

Common mistakes

❌ I'm going to Rome
✓ I'm going to Rome
❌ I live in Italy
✓ I live in Italy
❌ I'm leaving Luca's
✓ I'm going out with Luca
