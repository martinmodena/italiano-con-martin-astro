What are they?

Definite articles indicate a specific person or thing: the book, the house, the students.

The choice depends on three things: gender, number and initial sound of the word.

the
common singular masculine
the book
I
in front of s+consonant, z, ps, gn, x, y
the student
the
feminine singular
the house
the
before a vowel
the friend, the friend

🇬🇧 Why do we say "the student"?
Because “student” begins with st. In front of s + consonant we use I.

Examples to observe

il tavolo → i tavoli
lo studente → gli studenti
lo zaino → gli zaini
l’amico → gli amici
la porta → le porte
l’amica → le amiche

Mistakes that almost everyone makes

❌ il studente✓ lo studente
❌ i studenti✓ gli studenti
❌ il amico✓ l’amico
❌ le amico✓ gli amici

Makeup: the plural of I and of the it is always masculine him.
