In montagna

You go in montagna (to the mountains) but al mare (to the seaside), and in montagna also says where you are: siamo in montagna. Before a name Italian uses il monte: il Monte Bianco, il Monte Rosa; ranges are plural: le Alpi, gli Appennini, le Dolomiti. You fai un’escursione (go hiking) or una passeggiata (go for a walk), you sali in cima (climb to the top), you vai a sciare (go skiing). Gli sci are the skis (plural, there are two), lo sci is the sport: mi piace lo sci. Beach words are in the lesson The sea; the marmot, the eagle and the deer in Animals.
