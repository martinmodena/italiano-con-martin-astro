Words for everyday life

These adjectives describe character and mood, and are mostly used for people (Mia nonna è generosa, Sono triste, Sei troppo distratto): the animals are just a fun way to remember them. Many adjectives have two forms: pigro (masculine) and pigra (feminine); those ending in -e, like felice and gentile, have only one. The links between animals and traits are mostly stereotypes, as in Italian sayings (furbo come una volpe, “sly as a fox”; testardo come un mulo, “stubborn as a mule”): they are not scientific facts.
