A small note

Many adjectives have two forms: pigro (masculine) and pigra (feminine). Those ending in -e, like veloce and forte, have only one. The links between animals and traits are mostly stereotypes, as in Italian sayings (furbo come una volpe, “sly as a fox”; testardo come un mulo, “stubborn as a mule”): they are not scientific facts.
