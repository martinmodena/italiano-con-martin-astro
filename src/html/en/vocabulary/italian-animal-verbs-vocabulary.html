A small note

In Italian the infinitive ends in -are, -ere or -ire; in a sentence the verb changes its ending (abbaiare → il cane abbaia). Verbs like arrotolarsi and arrampicarsi end in -si: they are reflexive. For animal sounds Italian has a special verb for each animal (abbaiare, miagolare, muggire…): it is best to learn them together.
