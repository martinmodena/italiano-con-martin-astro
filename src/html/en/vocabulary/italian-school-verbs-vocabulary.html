Studiare o imparare?

Studiare (to study) is the work with books, imparare (to learn) is the result: studio tanto, ma non imparo niente! (I study a lot, but I don’t learn anything!). Before an infinitive it is imparare a: imparo a nuotare (I’m learning to swim). A question is “made”: posso fare una domanda? (may I ask a question?); you also “make” i compiti (homework), un esperimento, merenda (a snack). Essere promosso and essere bocciato agree with the speaker: sono stata promossa, sono stati bocciati. You graduate in a subject: mi sono laureata in lettere (I graduated in humanities). Objects and places are in the lesson School; insegnare (to teach) and chiacchierare (to chat) in Relationship verbs.
