Come ti senti?

To say how you feel, use essere or sentirsi with the adjective agreeing with you: sono contento (a man), sono contenta (a woman), mi sento stanca; felice, triste, entusiasta and impaziente have one form for both. Some emotions take avere: ho paura (I’m afraid), ho vergogna or mi vergogno (I’m ashamed). Careful with a false friend: before a trip or an exam you are emozionato, not eccitato, which has a sexual meaning. Solo means alone (vivo da solo) and also lonely (mi sento solo). A mood passes, character stays: oggi sono nervoso (I’m nervous today) is not è una persona nervosa (he’s a nervous person); character adjectives are in the lesson Animal personality. In the exercise you can also write the noun: la felicità, la paura, la rabbia.
