A mobile is a cupboard!

False friend number one: il mobile is a piece of furniture; your mobile phone is il cellulare or il telefonino. Italians answer the phone with Pronto?, not “hello”. You chiami qualcuno with no preposition (chiamo mia madre), but you telefoni a qualcuno. A dead phone è scarico and you lo metti in carica; careful, the same verb means “to download”: scarico un’app. “No signal” is non c’è campo, a voice note un vocale, a phone case la cover and speakerphone il vivavoce. Many English words are used as they are (il tablet, il router, la password, la chat), but they get an article and a gender. And to end a call: ci sentiamo! (talk soon!).
