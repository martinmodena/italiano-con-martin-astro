A camera is a room!

Two false friends for every English-speaking traveller. La camera is a hotel room (una camera doppia, a double room): the camera you take photos with is la macchina fotografica. And la coincidenza is not a coincidence but your connecting train or flight: ho perso la coincidenza, I missed my connection. “To miss” a train is perdere, literally “to lose”: ho perso il treno. You prendere (take) trains and planes, salire (get on) and scendere (get off). “To pack” is fare la valigia, “to make the suitcase”. On regional trains and buses you must validate your paper ticket in the obliteratrice (timbrare il biglietto), or you can be fined. Cities take a, countries in: vado a Roma, vado in Italia. A return ticket is andata e ritorno. And to someone leaving, say Buon viaggio!
