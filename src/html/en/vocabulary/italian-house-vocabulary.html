A casa

“At home” is a casa, with no article: sono a casa (I’m at home), vado a casa (I’m going home). “My home” is casa mia, with no article and the possessive after the noun. La stanza is any room; la camera is usually the bedroom: una casa di quattro stanze (a four-room house), la camera dei bambini (the children’s bedroom). Il bagno is the bathroom, but fare il bagno means having a bath or swimming in the sea. Il piano terra is the ground floor, so il primo piano is one floor up, as in British English. The plural of il lenzuolo is le lenzuola. Kitchen and living-room objects are in the lessons The kitchen and The living room.
