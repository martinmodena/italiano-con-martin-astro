Money is plural: i soldi

English “money” is singular, but Italian soldi is always plural: i soldi sono nel portafoglio, quanti soldi hai? “Change” has two translations: the money you get back is il resto (tenga il resto, keep the change), small coins are gli spiccioli; la moneta is just a coin. Careful with il biglietto: it’s a ticket, while a banknote is la banconota. Il conto is the bill in a restaurant (il conto, per favore), and also a bank account. The receipt is lo scontrino, a cash machine and a debit card are both il bancomat, and the card reader is il POS. “To do the shopping” for food is fare la spesa; for clothes and fun it’s fare shopping. The sales are i saldi, in January and July. And to ask the price: Quanto costa?
