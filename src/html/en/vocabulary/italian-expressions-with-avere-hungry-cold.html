Ho fame, not “I am hungry”

The big trap for English speakers: where English uses “to be”, Italian uses avere, “to have”. “I am hungry” is ho fame (I have hunger), “I am cold” is ho freddo, “I am afraid” is ho paura, “I am in a hurry” is ho fretta, and “you are right” is hai ragione, “I was wrong” avevo torto. Sono affamato exists, but it means “I’m starving”. Because what follows avere is a noun, “very” is molta or molto, agreeing with it: ho molta fame, ho molto freddo. And keep three sentences apart: ho caldo (I feel hot), fa caldo (it’s hot weather), il tè è caldo (the tea is hot). “I need” is ho bisogno di: ho bisogno di aiuto.
