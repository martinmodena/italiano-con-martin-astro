“To know” is two verbs: sapere and conoscere

Use sapere for facts and skills (so dov’è, I know where it is; so nuotare, I can swim) and conoscere for people and places (conosco Marco, conosco Roma). Ci siamo conosciuti a Roma means “we met in Rome”. “To ask” and “to ask for” are both chiedere, with no preposition for the thing: chiedo il conto (I ask for the bill). “Say” and “tell” are both dire, and the person takes a: l’ho detto a mia madre. “To tell a story” is raccontare. Careful: ricordare a qualcuno is “to remind”, ricordarsi di “to remember”. Discutere can also mean “to argue”. “To realise” is accorgersi or rendersi conto, not realizzare (which means to make real). And “to lie” is dire una bugia or mentire.
