With the body, Italian uses “the”, not “my”

With parts of the body Italian usually uses the definite article, not a possessive: Mi lavo le mani (not “le mie mani”), Alzo il braccio, Mi fa male la testa. Verbs ending in -si, like pettinarsi and lavarsi, are reflexive: the action goes back to the person (mi pettino, si pettina). Some cards have several words (lavarsi le mani, sentire con le orecchie) because the verb alone is not enough: these are the expressions people really use. Verbs about the whole body (crescere, invecchiare, guarire…) have a card but no row in the exercises.
