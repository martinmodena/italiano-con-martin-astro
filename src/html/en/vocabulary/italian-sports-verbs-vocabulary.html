Sports verbs

Giocare a calcio, fare yoga

Ball sports are “played” with giocare a and no article: gioco a calcio (I play football), a tennis, a pallavolo (volleyball). Most other sports are “done” with fare: fare yoga, fare surf, fare pesi (to lift weights), fare jogging; a few take andare: andare a cavallo (to ride a horse), andare in palestra (to go to the gym). Allenarsi is reflexive (mi alleno: I train myself), allenare is not (l’allenatore allena la squadra: the coach trains the team). In the passato prossimo, arrivare and reflexive verbs take essere, and the participle agrees: Giulia è arrivata prima (Giulia came first), mi sono infortunata (I got injured, said by a woman), ci siamo allenati (we trained). Segnare (to score) is used on its own or with un gol, un punto, un canestro. Sports words (the ball, the referee, the finish line, the medal…) are in the lesson Sports.
